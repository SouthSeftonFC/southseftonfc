Tournament 12 July 2026

U9s Lift The Summer Cup in Thrilling Final

Upload tournament photo here

An incredible weekend of football saw our Under 9s battle through tough competition to bring home the silverware at the annual Sefton Summer Tournament. The boys showed fantastic resilience across the two-day event, topping their group with three wins and a draw.

In Sunday's knockout stages, they displayed some of the best passing football we've seen all season. The semi-final was a tense affair, ending in a penalty shootout where our goalkeeper made two spectacular saves to send us through.

The final was a showcase of grassroots football at its finest. Despite going a goal down early in the first half, the team rallied together, equalised just before the break, and scored a dramatic late winner in the dying minutes to seal a 2-1 victory.

A massive congratulations to all the players, coaches, and parents who supported them all weekend. Up the Borough!

Club Update 05 July 2026

New Kits Sponsored by Local Business

A massive thank you to our new main sponsors who have generously provided stunning new home and away kits for all four of our competitive squads ahead of the upcoming season.

Running a grassroots football club relies heavily on the support of the local community, and this sponsorship ensures that our players can step onto the pitch feeling proud and looking professional. The new design sticks to our traditional Borough Blue and Yellow, but introduces a modern fade pattern that the players absolutely love.

If you own a local business and are interested in supporting grassroots football, we still have availability for training jacket sponsors and pitch-side banners. Please get in touch via our Contact page for more information.

Recruitment 28 June 2026

Open Trials Announced for Upcoming Season

We are excited to announce that South Sefton FC will be hosting open trials for enthusiastic new players looking to join our U7 and U11 squads for the new season.

Our club is built on a philosophy of development, teamwork, and fun. Whether your child is taking their first steps into competitive football or looking for a new challenge, all abilities are welcome to attend.

Trial Details:

  • Venue: Mill Dam Field
  • Date: Next Saturday & Sunday
  • What to bring: Boots, shin pads, and a water bottle

Please ensure you register your interest beforehand by sending us a message through our Contact page. We can't wait to see some new faces down at the pitch!

About South Sefton FC

Our History

Founded with a passion to provide local children a safe, fun, and competitive environment to play football, South Sefton FC has grown from a single squad to multiple teams competing across various age groups. We pride ourselves on being a community-first club.

Our Philosophy

Development over winning. While we compete fiercely on the pitch, our primary goal is the physical, mental, and social development of our young players. We believe in respect for referees, opponents, and each other.

Proud Supporters

South Sefton FC is incredibly proud to support the Darby Rimmer MND Foundation. We stand together in the fight against Motor Neurone Disease, raising awareness and vital funds to support those affected and fund research for a cure.

Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At South Sefton Football Club, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our Contact Form or Trial Registration Form, we collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details regarding the player. This information is sent directly to our club email and is solely used for communication and club administration.

2. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies purely to ensure the basic functionality of the site (such as remembering if you have dismissed the cookie banner). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

3. Data Sharing

We will never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with relevant club officials (managers, safeguarding officers) when absolutely necessary.
